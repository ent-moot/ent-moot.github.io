Location

Abbotskerswell is a lovely base: village quiet, countryside close, and quick hops to market towns and the coast. Ideal for exploring the best of South Devon without feeling rushed.

Easy days out

  • Newton Abbot: handy shops, trains, and essentials close by.
  • Totnes: independent cafés, historic streets, riverside walks, and markets.
  • Dartmoor: wide-open views, tors, and miles of walking routes.
  • South Devon coast: Teignmouth, Shaldon, and beyond for beaches and seafood.

Whether you’re after coastal swims, woodland walks, or a slow afternoon in a café, this is a brilliant base for it all.
